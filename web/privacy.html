Air Guitar Hero: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Air Guitar Hero lets you play air guitar using your device's camera. We built it so that your camera feed and everything about you stays on your device.

Camera

The app uses the front camera to see your body position, so your avatar can copy your moves and your hand movements can play music. The video is processed only on your device, in real time. It is never recorded, stored, uploaded or shared with anyone, including us.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect, store or transmit any personal information.

Purchases

Guitar is free. Other instruments can be unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase, which is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether your account owns the upgrade so it can unlock it on your devices.

Data stored on your device

The app remembers your settings (instrument, avatar, guitar, chord set, sound, left-handed mode and camera preview) and whether you've unlocked the full version on your device so they are there next time. You can clear them by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/JPPotgieter/AirGuitar.
