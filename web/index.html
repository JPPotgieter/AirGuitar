Play on your TV

  1. Connect to your TV: tap below and pick your Chromecast or smart TV. Your screen and sound will appear on the TV.
  2. TV mode turns the app sideways so it fills the TV.
  3. Stand your phone sideways under the TV, camera facing you, and step back about 2 m so it can see you from the waist up.

Can't find it? Swipe down from the top of the screen and tap Cast, Smart View or Screen mirroring.
